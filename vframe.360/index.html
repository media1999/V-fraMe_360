YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Who can join V-FraMe 360?
Students from all departments with passion for media, photography, videography, editing, or content creation can join.
What activities does the media team handle?
We manage photography, videography, editing, dubbing, event coverage, social media promotions, and creative branding projects.
Do members get hands-on experience?
Yes! Members gain real-time experience by covering college events, workshops, cultural programs, and technical fests.
Can beginners join the media team?
Absolutely. Training, mentorship, and teamwork help beginners improve their creative and technical skills.
What equipment does V-FraMe 360 use?
Our team works with professional cameras, drones, lighting setups, editing software, and audio equipment.
How can we collaborate with V-FraMe 360?
Departments, clubs, and event organizers can contact the media team through the connect section for collaborations and event coverage.
